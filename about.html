About The Cat's Meow

A little family, a lot of love for cats.

We are a family-owned cat sitting business with a passion for providing the best care to your furry friends. Here's a little information about us:

  • Caring owners of Minnie (rescued from the Seattle Humane Society)
  • Our names are Blake and Marlowe; we are 13 (Blake) and 10 (Marlowe) years old.
  • We have been cat sitting for 7 years. We live in the Issaquah Highlands.
  • Our parents accompany us on each visit.
  • We LOVE cats! We will take care of your cat as if it is our own!
